Which research directions are drawing attention, and who is working on them? The ranked inventory includes 35 cross-area conference series alongside AI-facing CORE, CCF, and TH-CPL venues. Organizer submissions and acceptances stay in their separately reviewed ledger. Verified main-track totals, bibliography-only fallback counts, observed AI matches, and sampled AI papers are different measures, each with its own source and denominator. The local bibliography backfill covers 2020-2026, but most main-track totals remain unverified; missing years and zero OpenAlex source matches are not zero venue publications. Papers without verified first-author affiliation are excluded from the sector curves.
