Score method

Editorial 0–6 proxy, not measured business adoption. GitHub stars and pushes are repository signals only.

score = 6 × sum(weight × available component value) / sum(available weights)

evidence coverage = sum(available weights) / sum(selected weights)

Minimum evidence: 2 available components and 55% coverage, or 4 including code and reviewed adoption or maintenance with 50% coverage.

Unknown evidence is omitted, never scored as zero. Open a paper in the ledger to inspect its own score audit.

05 / METHOD

What this can, and cannot, tell us

The catalog includes 35 new cross-area series in systems, architecture, compilers, parallel/distributed computing, and software engineering, alongside the earlier AI-facing venues. Actual CORE 2023, CCF 2026, and TH-CPL memberships are linked by venue. Verified main-track totals require a complete research-track index; DBLP bibliography counts remain separately labelled. Observed AI matches are a subset of scanned eligible records, not all AI papers. The paper ledger is a capped, AI-matched sample and is not a conference census. Missing venue-years remain gaps.

Official-only papers keep citation and affiliation fields unknown. Paper-specific affiliations determine first-author/team sectors, not leadership. PMLR software links and paper-backed repository reviews establish code links; GitHub stars and latest push are dated snapshots. Actual product integration or business deployment and sustained maintenance require separate, specific review evidence. Technical reports outside proceedings remain excluded.
