05 / METHOD

What this can, and cannot, tell us

The snapshot uses OpenAlex metadata for six source-identified conference series, with venue inclusion checked against CORE or CCF. It samples up to 25 earliest-dated OpenAlex conference-paper records per venue-year. Source mapping, paper type, regular-track status, topic relevance, and affiliations can be wrong or incomplete. Broad-venue papers pass a disclosed AI-keyword rule; AI-native venues are included by source match. Counts are not conference acceptance totals.

First-author sector comes from paper-specific affiliation types. A cross-sector team is not automatically jointly led. Leadership, funding, compute, awards, and code remain unknown until a sourced review is committed. Technical reports outside proceedings are excluded, including many frontier-model releases. Citation percentile is OpenAlex's field/year-normalized signal; citation counts can change and recent papers have less time to accrue them. Review the reproducibility notes and review file.
